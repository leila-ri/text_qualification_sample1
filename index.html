Script 1: Pre-Form Survey Qualification
Lead filled out basic form (name, contact, damage type) but NOT detailed survey yet. You text to ask about roof age and damage to qualify before sending inspection.
Initial Flow (Same for all scenarios)

✓ Key Points for Script 1 (Pre-Form)

  • No form data yet: Lead only filled out basic contact info. You need to qualify them on roof age and damage to decide if they're a fit.
  • Timing: Message 1 = immediate (5 min). Message 2 after they confirm text is ok. Ask all 3 questions at once.
  • Qualify on: Roof age (15+ years ideal), damage type/severity, timeline urgency (less than 6 months).
  • If vague: Offer a call after 1-2 text exchanges. Don't push for more text answers.
  • Always book inspection: Qualified leads get booked. Unqualified leads get soft close with "save our number" message.

✓ Key Points for Script 2 (Post-Form)

  • Form data available: You already have roof age & damage details from the survey form. You're just asking about replacement vs repair, financing options, and timeline urgency.
  • Timing: Message 1 = immediate (5 min of form submission). Message 2 after they confirm they have time. Ask all 3 questions at once to move fast.
  • Key questions to qualify: Replacement or repair? How paying (cash/financing/insurance)? Timeline urgency?
  • If they ask complex questions: Don't try to answer financing details in text. Offer a quick call: "Easier to walk through this in 5 minutes" and switch to phone.
  • Insurance claims: If they mention insurance, always offer inspection to document and help with claim. Position your inspector as their advocate.
  • Repair vs Replacement: If they want repair but roof is 15+ years with damage, gently suggest full inspection to see if replacement makes more sense long-term.
  • Always book the inspection: Even if timeline is vague, get them scheduled. The inspector determines if it's repair or replacement based on assessment.
  • Get phone number: Always end with "what's the best number to reach you?" before sending time confirmation next morning.
